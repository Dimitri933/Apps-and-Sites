FAQ

Do I need a coding background?

No. Lessons are built for product managers — concepts are explained through architecture, trade-offs, and decisions, not code.

How long is each lesson?

4–8 minutes. Every lesson is scoped to one clear idea so you can watch it between meetings.

What's actually tested?

Scenario-based questions and mock interviews that check whether you can apply a concept under realistic conditions — not multiple-choice trivia.

Is this updated as the field changes?

Yes. RAG, evals, and RAGAS practices evolve quickly, and modules are revised as best practices shift.
